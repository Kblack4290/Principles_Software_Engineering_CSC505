## Optional extensions (dashed in the diagram)

- **Notification Service:** reminders and budget alerts, connected to the List Controller and the Price & Budget Service.
- **Price & Budget Service:** item prices, totals, and budget checks.
- **Purchase History Service:** records trips and frequent items, backed by the Purchase History Store.

## How to run

1. Open a terminal in this folder.
2. Run `python shopping_list_prototype.py` (or `python3 shopping_list_prototype.py`, depending on your environment).
